Privacy

Last updated October 10, 2026.

What you send us

When you book a job, the form sends your name, phone number, address, the job and the time you picked to our email through our form provider, Web3Forms. Crew applications send the details on that form the same way. We use them to schedule and do the work, and we never sell them.

Ads and Meta

We use pixels, web beacons and other storage technologies from third parties, including Meta, to collect or receive information from this website and elsewhere on the internet. Meta and other third parties use that information to provide measurement services and to target and deliver ads. When you book a job, this site tells Meta that a booking happened, without your job details. If Meta's advanced matching is on, contact details you type into our booking form, such as your name and phone number, are scrambled (hashed) in your browser before they are sent, and Meta says it uses them to match the booking to people on Meta and then discards them. Our crew application page does not use the Meta pixel.

You can opt out of ads based on this information:

  • In your Facebook or Instagram ad preferences: accountscenter.facebook.com/ad_preferences
  • With the Digital Advertising Alliance's WebChoices tool: optout.aboutads.info
  • By blocking or clearing cookies in your browser settings.

Meta explains how it uses cookies in its Cookies Policy.

Visitor counts

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies.

Questions

Email commonshomeservices@gmail.com and we'll answer, or delete your details if you ask.
